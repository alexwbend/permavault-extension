## Detailed description (max 16,000 chars)

```
Some pages matter too much to lose. An article you paid for. A post that might get edited or deleted. A page you need as evidence. Permavault lets you keep any page you can open in your browser, permanently.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Open the page you want to keep.
2. Click the Permavault icon, then "Archive this page".
3. Permavault records the page inside your own browser session, exactly as you see it, including pages behind logins and subscriptions you have access to.
4. The capture is sealed with a cryptographic signature, stored permanently on the Arweave network, and added to your vault at app.permavault.xyz.

Every capture comes with a signed record and an exhibit PDF, so you can prove later what the page said and when you archived it. Captures are public permanent records by design, and the certificate states honestly that the page was captured in your own browser session.

WHO IT IS FOR

- Journalists and researchers who need a source preserved as it was.
- Legal and compliance teams collecting web evidence.
- Anyone who paid for an article and wants a permanent copy they can share.
- Anyone tired of link rot.

WHAT IT COSTS

The extension is free. Captures are prepaid units on your Permavault account, one unit per capture up to 100 MB. You can sign in with your Arweave keyfile; email sign-in is available on the web app.

YOUR PRIVACY

Nothing leaves your browser until you click Archive. The extension records a page only at your explicit request, sends that capture to your Permavault account, and keeps a local copy in the extension's library on your machine. We do not track your browsing and we do not sell data. Full policy: https://app.permavault.xyz/privacy

OPEN SOURCE

The Permavault browser extension is open source (AGPL) and builds on Webrecorder's ArchiveWeb.page capture engine. Source: https://github.com/alexwbend/permavault-extension
```
